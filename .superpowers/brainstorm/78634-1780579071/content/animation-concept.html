How should the "Expand to Full Layer" animation work?

When you click a summary item, how do you want the animation to behave?

1

Metric Card Expands to Fill Dashboard

The specific summary card you click (e.g. Total Assets) smoothly grows to fill the main white dashboard area, hiding the other summary cards. The sidebar remains visible.

2

Main Card Expands to Full Screen

The main white dashboard card breaks out of its layout and grows to fill the entire browser window (covering the sidebar and top navigation) for maximum focus on the details.
